There is always another sound to find.

06 / NEED TO KNOW

Quick answers

Before you pull up.

Still unsure? Message the club on Instagram and ask the people who run it.

When and where does UGRC meet?+

Wednesdays at lunch in room A101.

Do I need to know underground rap already?+

No. The whole point is finding more to hear. Curiosity is enough.

Do I have to bring a song?+

No. Bring one if you want, or just show up and listen.

What are the room rules?+

Respect the classroom and everyone in it, do not take or display photos of other members, and clean up after yourself.

What is UGRC building toward?+

A tight-knit community of underground fans and a club the whole school takes seriously. Good room behavior may also unlock a member-voted monthly treat.

How do I keep up with the club?+

Follow @dnhs_ugrc or join the Discord linked below.
