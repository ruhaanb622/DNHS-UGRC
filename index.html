01 / WHO WE ARE

There is always another sound to find.

Underground rap lives outside the obvious. UGRC is where Del Norte students can pull up, trade discoveries, talk music, and be fully themselves.

You do not need a perfect playlist or a deep catalog. Bring a track, bring an opinion, or just bring your lunch. Curiosity is the only cover charge.
